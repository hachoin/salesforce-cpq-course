Salesforce CPQ Course Overview

The Salesforce CPQ course is designed to provide practical, job-oriented expertise in configuring and managing Salesforce CPQ solutions to automate and optimize the sales quoting and pricing process. As part of the world’s leading cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform, Salesforce CPQ is widely used by organizations to streamline product configuration, pricing, discounting, and quote generation. This course introduces learners to the Salesforce CPQ ecosystem, core CPQ concepts, and the Lightning Experience interface used by modern enterprises.


Throughout the course, participants gain hands-on experience in key Salesforce CPQ areas such as product configuration, pricing rules, discount schedules, and quote templates. Learners work with CPQ objects including Products, Price Books, Quotes, and Contracts, and learn how to automate sales processes using Salesforce tools and approval workflows. They also develop the ability to generate professional quotes and reports that provide valuable business insights, enabling accurate and efficient decision-making within an organization.


By the end of the program, learners will be prepared to handle real-time Salesforce CPQ implementation scenarios and confidently pursue the Salesforce CPQ Certification. The course emphasizes practical configuration, live project exposure, and interview preparation, enabling participants to step into roles such as Salesforce CPQ Consultant, CPQ Analyst, or Salesforce Functional Support Executive with confidence and industry-ready skills.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Salesforce CPQ Consultant do?
A Salesforce CPQ Consultant manages and customizes the Salesforce CPQ solution for an organization. They configure products, pricing, and discount rules, automate the quote-to-cash process, and ensure accurate and efficient quote generation. They also manage user access, approvals, reports, and dashboards related to sales performance, ensuring the CPQ system supports business requirements effectively.
Do I need coding knowledge to learn Salesforce CPQ?
No. Salesforce CPQ is a low-code role where most work is done using configuration tools like product rules, pricing rules, and approval workflows rather than traditional programming.
Is Salesforce CPQ Consultant a good career in 2026?
Yes. Salesforce CPQ is a highly valuable career path in 2026 as organizations increasingly adopt CPQ solutions to automate their sales and quoting processes. Salesforce CPQ professionals are in strong global demand, with excellent salary growth, career opportunities, and long-term job stability across multiple industries.
What skills are required to become a Salesforce CPQ Consultant?
You need to understand CRM and sales process concepts, Salesforce CPQ data model (Products, Price Books, Quotes), pricing and discount rules, security settings, automation tools, and reports & dashboards, along with a basic understanding of business workflows.
How long does it take to learn Salesforce CPQ and get a job?
With proper training and hands-on practice, most learners can become job-ready in 2 to 3 months and start applying for Salesforce CPQ Consultant or CPQ Analyst roles with confidence.
